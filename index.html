I'm Trevor, an Electrical & Computer Engineering student at Duke, concentrating in machine learning. I spent last summer at Hong Kong's Applied Science & Technology Research Institute validating 5G radio performance, and before Duke I co-founded two startups and captained a robotics team to a world championship. I like projects with a physical output — something that lights up, moves, or reminds someone to take their medication.
